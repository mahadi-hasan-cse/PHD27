# CS Faculty and PhD Hiring Research

Date: 2026-09-04

## Scope and direct answer

The site directory covers 7,451 active research-faculty records mapped across the 100 universities already in the Fall 2027 application database. Identity, affiliation, homepage, Google Scholar, and ORCID fields are drawn from the current CSRankings dataset. Public professional contact and lab/GitHub fields are included only when present or separately verified. A missing field is not inferred.

“Hiring” is a narrow evidence category: it requires a public faculty or lab page that explicitly says PhD students are being recruited. A functioning lab, recent publication, grant, or assistant-professor title is not treated as hiring evidence. Three records currently meet the explicit-evidence rule; the remainder say “Not publicly confirmed.”

## Material limitations

CSRankings is an active research-faculty dataset, not a contractual HR roster. It may omit teaching faculty, recent hires, emeriti, visitors, or researchers outside its venue-based inclusion method. Departmental rosters change continuously. Hiring statements can become stale without a visible date, and “Not publicly confirmed” must not be read as “not hiring.”

## Stop rationale

The exhaustive identity layer has complete university coverage in the selected research-faculty source. Hiring verification stopped at explicit, current public evidence discovered in the first targeted search waves; expanding hiring evidence across 7,451 people requires ongoing monitoring because the underlying state changes and many faculty do not publish a definitive status.
